Scout privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026.

Scout is a private assistant for one household. Its only users are the two people who run it.

What Scout accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): to spot deadlines, bookings and receipts. Scout cannot send, delete or change email.
  • Google Calendar: free/busy only from the users' own calendars, and its own separate calendars where it adds plans after a user approves them.
  • Contacts (read-only): for birthday reminders.

How the data is handled

  • Everything runs on a private home server. Data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • Data from Google is used only to provide these features to the household, and is not transferred to third parties except as needed to run the assistant itself.
  • Scout's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted on request.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
